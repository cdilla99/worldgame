Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026

Playing without signing in

You can play without an account. Nicknames, avatars, settings, and guest progress are saved in this browser. Guest progress is not uploaded to our account database. Anyone using the same browser may be able to see device profiles.

Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your account identifier and basic profile information, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. We use Google and Supabase to authenticate you. Earthling does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, contacts, or Drive.

Supabase stores your account information and linked game records, including scores, statistics, country-review progress, and Daily Puzzle guesses. This lets you restore progress and use it on another device. Your browser also keeps local progress and a sign-in session.

Services used by the game

Netlify hosts Earthling. Google provides optional sign-in, and Supabase provides account storage. Country flags, outlines, and landmark content may load from FlagCDN, jsDelivr, GitHub, and Wikimedia services. These services receive connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser contacts them. Hosting and authentication services may keep operational and security logs.

Earthling does not include advertising or third-party analytics scripts. We use account information and progress to operate the game, provide saves, and protect the service.

Keeping and deleting data

Local records remain until you clear them or your browser removes them. Clearing browser data can erase device-only progress and sign you out; it does not delete cloud saves. Signing out or removing Google's permission also does not delete saved records.

We keep cloud account records and progress until you request deletion. To request access, correction, or deletion, email info@thinkabacus.ca from the email linked to your account. We may ask you to confirm ownership. Service backups and security logs may remain according to the providers' retention processes.

Contact

Earthling is operated by ThinkAbacus. For privacy questions, including questions about a child's information, email info@thinkabacus.ca.
